Privacy policy

AoE2 DE tech tree filter does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sell or share personal information. There is no analytics service and no advertising.

The only thing stored on your device is the language you pick (English, Russian, or Polish). That value stays in this browser's local storage under the key aoe2-lang. Clearing site data removes it. The page does not send that choice to a server of its own.

Inside Discord, the page does not request your Discord identity, email, or messages, and it does not store them. Discord still knows that you opened the activity, under Discord's own privacy policy.

The files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary connection logs for that hosting. That is described in GitHub's privacy statement.
